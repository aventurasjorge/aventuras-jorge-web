Flex Zone Boundary Drawer

1. Find the area

If a result has a real official boundary on file (a township, borough, or city), a "Use boundary" button appears next to it — click that instead of hand-drawing. Otherwise (informal place names like Diamondtown, or a plain address) clicking the result just pans the map there so you can trace it by hand below.
Jump straight to one of the four zones that need manual tracing:

2. Draw the boundary

Click the map to drop points for the current part. Click the first point again (or press "Finish part") to close the ring. Once points exist — hand-drawn, imported, or loaded from an official boundary — every point on every part can be dragged to nudge it, right-clicked to delete it, or you can double-click an edge to insert a new point there. This works on finished parts too, not just the one you're actively drawing.
